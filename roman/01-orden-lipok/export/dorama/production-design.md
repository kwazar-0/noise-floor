## Общая эстетика

**Period**: 1992–1993, Киев (ядро) + международные локации (орбита)  
**Genre**: Psychological noir thriller  
**Visual references**:
- **Cinematography**: Tarkovsky (Stalker, Mirror), Fincher (Zodiac, Mindhunter), Villeneuve (Prisoners)
- **Color**: Desaturated, muted palette, institutional greens/grays, amber accent lights
- **Texture**: Worn surfaces, condensation, rain, fabric detail, dust in light
- **Aspect ratio**: 2.39:1 (scope) for cinematic feel

**Key principles**:
1. **Spaces judge people** — architecture as psychological pressure
2. **Light sources are practical** — no unmotivated beauty lighting
3. **Textures tell time** — nothing is new, everything worn
4. **Evidence is object** — capsule, documents, stamps close-ups

- Apron
- Worn dress
- Slippers

**Петро**:
- Undershirt
- Worn sweater

- Elegant secretary style

- Consultant: 1990s Ukraine fashion
- Fabrics: natural, worn (no synthetics that look new)
- Colors: muted, faded
- No contemporary cuts or accessories

## Cinematography

### Camera package
- **Body**: Arri Alexa Mini или RED Komodo (digital, film grain in post)
- **Lenses**: 
  - 24mm (wide establishing)
  - 50mm (medium coverage)
  - 85mm (close-ups, shallow DOF)
  - Occasional 135mm (extreme close-ups)
- **Movement**:
  - Tripod static (Tarkovsky scenes: cell, СИТО)
  - Handheld (tension: escape, moral crisis)
  - Dolly slow push-ins (psychological pressure)
  - Steadicam (corridor walks)

### Lighting approach
- **Motivated practicals**: every light source visible or justified
- **No beauty light**: harsh institutional або single-source intimate
- **Color temperature**:
  - Tungsten 3200K (salon amber, warm interiors)
  - Fluorescent 5600K (СИТО, offices)
  - Daylight 5600K (exteriors)
- **Negative fill**: агрессивно использовать для noir shadows

### Color grade
- **LUT**: Desaturated, lifted blacks slightly, crushed highlights
- **Palette**: See above per location
- **Grain**: 35mm film grain overlay, medium intensity
- **Vignette**: Subtle на corners для scope

## Sound Design

### Key sound motifs
1. **Radiator hum** — Episode 1, returns in СИТО (machine undertone)
2. **Breathing in sync** — Cult, later becomes kулер hum
3. **Rain** — Episode 1, returns as emotional motif
4. **CRT monitors hum** — Episodes 10, 15
5. **Матричный printer треск** — СИТО scenes
6. **Жако крик** — вместо телефона motif
7. **Четыре комнаты слоями** — Episode 7 finale (жалюзи, kулеры, мазут, паркет sounds overlap)

### Music
- **Score**: Minimal, electronic undertones (Jóhann Jóhannsson, Ben Salisbury влияние)
- **Source music**: 
  - Vinyl records (salon)
  - Ukrainian folk-rock (Петро singing Скрябіна)
  - Radio static
- **Silence**: агрессивно использовать

## Post-production

### VFX
- Minimal
- Remove modern elements (signs, cars)
- CRT screen content (green text animation)
- Breath fog (cold exteriors)
- Rain enhancement
- (Optional) De-aging if needed

### ADR
- Билингвизм: русский + украинский simultaneous on set
- Loop group для cult breathing sync

### Final deliverables
- **Master**: 2.39:1, 4K, HDR (Dolby Vision)
- **Streaming**: 1080p SDR
- **Subtitles**: English, Ukrainian, Russian
- **Audio**: 5.1 surround + Atmos mix

## Budget considerations

**High-cost items**:
- Period vehicles (Москвич и др.)
- СИТО computer set build + period computers
- Salon set build
- International locations (Turkey, Austria, Israel, Hong Kong)
- Animal wrangler (parrot)
- Period costume authentic sourcing

**Cost-saving options**:
- Shoot Kyiv locations practical (local)
- Build СИТО and Salon sets, reuse
- CGI exteriors для international (cheaper than location)
- Animatronic parrot for close-ups, real bird для wide shots
- Ukraine filming incentives

**Estimated production budget** (live action):
- 8 episodes × $1.5–2M/ep = **$12–16M total** (mid-tier streaming series)

**AI-generated version**:
- Значительно дешевле ($500K–1M), но требует:
  - Character consistency tech
  - Voice actors (качество!)
  - Post-production compositing
  - Still requires practical sound design и score
